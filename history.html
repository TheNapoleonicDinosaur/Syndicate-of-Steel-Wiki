Chronicle of the Syndicate of Steel

Founding and Origins

The Syndicate of Steel was founded many years ago by MinisterOfCoal, who established the group on the Back2Basics Minecraft server. What began as a small operation has since grown to heights that even its founder did not believe reachable.

The SoS is a structured group built upon three core pillars: loyalty to the cause, unity among its members, and the principle that even the strongest leadership cannot stand without the support of its people. The management cannot stand if it does not have legs.

"Against all odds is where true victory begins." — MinisterOfCoal, Commissar General

Expansion

Over the years, the Syndicate expanded beyond its origins on Back2Basics, establishing a presence on Aethervale and becoming a fully multi-server operation. This expansion marked a turning point — transforming SoS from a single-server organisation into a cross-server collective with reach, reputation, and resources.

Ideology

The Syndicate of Steel operates on the belief that collective strength surpasses individual ambition. decisions and victories belong to the group — not to any one person. The council structure ensures that voices at every level are heard, while the chain of command ensures that the collective acts with purpose and efficiency.

"Production is Power." — Syndicate of Steel
