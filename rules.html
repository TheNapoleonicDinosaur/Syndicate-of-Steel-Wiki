Rules and Doctrine

Laws of the Collective · Code of Conduct

Mandatory Reading

All members of the Syndicate of Steel are required to know and follow these rules. Ignorance is not an excuse before the collective.

Article I — Loyalty

Rule 1.1 — All members owe their primary loyalty to the Syndicate of Steel above all other factions.

Rule 1.2 — Sharing base coordinates, strategic information, or internal matters with hostile or unaligned parties is grounds for immediate removal.

Article II — Conduct

Rule 2.1 — Griefing allied or neutral factions without officer authorisation is prohibited.

Rule 2.2 — If Commissar General is not online - Major General and Chief Council Officer have power.(under the condition they both agree with the decisions they make, and accept full responsiblity for the consequences).

Article III — Resources

Rule 3.1 — Major builds must be approved by a Council Officer or above before construction begins.

Article IV — Punishments

Minor violations: Warning.

Major violations: Demotion.

Treason: Removal and permanent ban.

"The Syndicate is built on trust. Those who break it are removed."
