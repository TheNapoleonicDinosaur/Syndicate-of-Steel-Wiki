Diplomatic Doctrine

The Syndicate of Steel maintains a structured approach to foreign relations. Alliances are forged on the basis of mutual benefit and shared values. State-funded organisations operate under the authority and resources of SoS, maintaining close ties to the collective's leadership.

Relations with unaligned factions remain subject to review by the Council. Any formal treaty requires approval at the Council Officer level or above.
