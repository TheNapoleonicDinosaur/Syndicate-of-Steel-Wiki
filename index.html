About the Syndicate

The Syndicate of Steel (SoS) is a Minecraft faction built on industrial power, collective discipline, and unwavering loyalty to the cause. Founded many years ago, it has grown into a multi-server operation spanning Back2Basics and Aethervale.

The SoS is a structured group based upon loyalty to the cause, unity, and having fun. The management cannot stand if it does not have any legs.
