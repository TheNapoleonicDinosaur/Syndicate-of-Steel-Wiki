Council Decrees

Official Laws and Mandates of the Syndicate of Steel

Binding on All Members

The following decrees represent the official body of law governing the Syndicate of Steel. All decrees are issued by the Commissar General and ratified by the Council. Ignorance of a decree is not a valid defence before the collective.

Active Decrees

Decree 001 Loyalty to the Collective Active
All members of the Syndicate of Steel owe their primary loyalty to the SoS above all other organisations, factions, or outside interests. No member may place the interests of an external party above those of the Syndicate.
Issued by: MinisterOfCoal, Commissar General Category: Loyalty
Decree 002 Prohibition of Unauthorised Griefing Active
Griefing allied or neutral factions without explicit authorisation from an officer of the Syndicate is strictly prohibited. Any act of unprovoked destruction against a non-hostile party must be approved through the proper chain of command before action is taken.
Issued by: MinisterOfCoal, Commissar General Category: Conduct
Decree 003 Protection of Strategic Information Active
Spying, leaking base coordinates, or sharing any strategic information with enemy or unaligned parties constitutes an act of treason against the Syndicate of Steel. This offence carries the maximum penalty of immediate and permanent removal from the SoS, with notification to all allied organisations.
Issued by: MinisterOfCoal, Commissar General Category: Security
Decree 004 Authorisation of SoS Branded Builds Active
All major builds representing or branded under the Syndicate of Steel must receive approval from an officer of the Syndicate prior to the commencement of construction. This decree exists to maintain the integrity and standards of SoS infrastructure across all servers.
Issued by: MinisterOfCoal, Commissar General Category: Infrastructure

Violations and Punishments

Violations of any active decree are assessed on a case-by-case basis by the Commissar General and the Council. Punishments are scaled to the severity and intent of the violation.

SeverityExamplesPunishment
Minor Unauthorised small-scale build, minor conduct breach Formal warning issued by a Council Officer
Moderate Repeated minor violations, unauthorised griefing Demotion of one or more ranks
Severe Theft, insubordination, repeated moderate violations Full demotion or expulsion from the SoS
Treason Leaking coordinates, spying, betrayal of the collective Immediate expulsion and blacklisting across all allied factions

Repealed Decrees

No decrees have been repealed at this time. Repealed decrees will be archived here for the historical record of the collective.
